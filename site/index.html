What is a SeedQR?

  • It is your private key. Scanning it is the same as typing in every word of the seed phrase.
  • Transcribe it by hand onto paper or steel, copying the grid from the device's own transcription screen.
  • Never photograph it, never make one digitally. Phones and computers sync, back up and cache without asking, and a copy you didn't know about is a copy you can't destroy.
